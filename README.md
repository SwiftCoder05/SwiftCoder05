### 03. 🧠 DocuChat — Intelligent Document Q&A

**Generative AI | Retrieval-Augmented Generation | LLM Applications**

A document-based question answering system that lets users upload PDFs, DOCX, TXT or Wikipedia content and ask questions in natural language.

- 📄 Loads and chunks documents from PDF, DOCX, TXT and Wikipedia sources.
- 🧬 Generates semantic embeddings with OpenAI's `text-embedding-3-small` and stores them in Pinecone for fast similarity search.
- 🔍 Retrieves the top-K relevant chunks and passes them to GPT-3.5-Turbo to generate grounded, context-aware answers.
- 💬 Supports multi-turn conversations with memory on a modular, scalable architecture.

**Tech Stack:** `Python` `LangChain` `OpenAI API` `Pinecone`
